Who we are

Why Sylvyn exists

Scotland generates more renewable electricity than it can use or move south. The scarce thing is not wind. It is land of the right scale, in the right place, with a real path onto that power. That is why Sylvyn exists: to develop the sites and associated energy infrastructure that turn surplus into load where the grid can actually take it.

Our work runs from site control through connection pathway and build readiness. Sylvyn Ltd is Scottish, registered in Edinburgh.
