In practice

Land, power pathway, delivery

Sylvyn works with institutional partners who need Scottish compute capacity at constrained grid nodes. The substance of that work is land positions of the right scale and place, a pathway onto capacity that can actually be secured, and a delivery programme from control through connection and build readiness.

Site detail, connection status, and programme timing are shared under NDA.
